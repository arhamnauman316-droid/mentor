# Mentor early-access launch copy

## Core message

**Before the wider launch, there's a chance to experience Mentor while it's still evolving.**

Build better habits. Turn goals into action. Reflect, learn, and move forward with a personal AI mentor. Your feedback can help shape the experience that comes next.

**Explore the early version. Help shape the future of Mentor.**

## Product Hunt description draft

Mentor is a personal AI mentor for people who want to turn intentions into consistent progress. Use AI conversations to think through challenges, set objectives, reflect in a journal, track progress, and build routines. Mentor is in early access, and feedback from early users helps guide what we improve next.

## Social post draft

I'm building Mentor — a personal AI mentor to help turn goals into action and make progress feel more intentional. I'm opening up an early version while I keep improving it. Try it, tell me what feels useful, and help shape what comes next.

## Honesty checklist

- Say "early access" only while Mentor is genuinely in an early-access phase.
- Do not invent a number of users, invite slots, waitlist size, endorsements, or a countdown.
- If access is limited, state the real limit and how it is enforced.
- Do not promise features that are not working in the current build.
